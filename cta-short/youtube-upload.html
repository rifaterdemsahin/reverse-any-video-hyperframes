Because your Google Cloud OAuth app is in Testing mode, Google blocks any account that is not explicitly listed under Test users — including your own Brand Channel owner account (Erdem). You do not need to submit for Google verification for personal CLI uploads.

  2. Scroll down to the Test users section and click + Add users.
  3. Enter the exact Google email address for Erdem (the account that owns Brand Channel UCSJyG3bTM7lnjMIZcV8C4OQ) and click Save.
  4. Re-run the 1-command private upload (takes effect immediately, no need to re-download client_secret.json):

STEP 3 · WHAT THE SCRIPT GUARANTEES

Built-In Safety & Shorts Verification

  • Channel Lock: Queries channels.list(mine=True) and aborts if the signed-in account does not match UCSJyG3bTM7lnjMIZcV8C4OQ.
  • Shorts Spec Check: Probes resolution (1080×1920 vertical) and duration (≤ 60s) via ffprobe and ensures #Shorts is present.
  • Resumable Chunked Upload: Streams in 8 MB chunks with live percentage progress and prints the direct Watch & Studio edit links.
  • Token Caching: First run opens Google Chrome once for OAuth consent and caches the refresh token at ~/.config/youtube-upload/token.json for headless future runs.
Security Rule: Never commit client_secret.json or token.json to git. Both stay outside the repository in ~/.config/youtube-upload/ with chmod 600.
