Why the original take felt quiet & music-heavy

In the original v7 mix, the raw camera voice averaged around -28 dBFS while music-bed.mp3 was mixed at volume=0.5 with weights=1 0.7. In v12/v13, we rescued it using exact phase-aligned subtraction (-17 dB music cut), but the cleanest long-term jump comes from fixing the voice level before mixing music.

OPTION 1 · HIGHEST QUALITY

Re-mix Directly from the Raw NINJAV Take (No Music Bleed)

Cost: $0 Impact: Huge Needs: NINJAV drive

When you have NINJAV_S001_S001_T020.MOV plugged in, process the clean voice channels (c2 + c3) first, normalize speech to -15 LUFS, and mix the music bed -22 dB below the voice instead of volume=0.5. 

# In scripts/video/greenscreen_short.sh (clean voice-first mix):
[0:a]pan=mono|c0=0.5*c2+0.5*c3,highpass=f=85,equalizer=f=2400:t=q:w=1.1:g=3,acompressor=threshold=-22dB:ratio=2.8:attack=10:release=180:makeup=10dB,loudnorm=I=-15:TP=-1.5:LRA=7,asplit=2[vsc][vmix];
[3:a]atrim=0:$dur,asetpts=PTS-STARTPTS,volume=0.14,equalizer=f=2400:t=q:w=1.0:g=-5,aformat=channel_layouts=stereo[mus];
[mus][vsc]sidechaincompress=threshold=0.02:ratio=8:attack=15:release=350[duck];
[vmix]aformat=channel_layouts=stereo[vm];
[vm][duck]amix=inputs=2:weights=1 0.35:normalize=0,alimiter=limit=0.95[a]
OPTION 2 · AI STUDIO POLISH

Run the Voice Stem Through Neural Voice Enhancement

Cost: Free – $15/mo Impact: Studio Condenser Sound Effort: 1 Minute

Neural speech enhancers remove room echo, hiss, and lav-mic muddiness without the chopping artifacts of a traditional noise gate:

  • Adobe Podcast Enhance Speech (v2): Upload the extracted voice WAV/MP3 and set strength to 65–80% (100% can sound overly synthetic; 70% sounds like a Shure SM7B in a treated booth).
  • ElevenLabs Audio Isolator: Strips background noise/music cleanly via API or web UI — easy to automate in your script pipeline.
  • DaVinci Resolve / Apple Voice Isolation: Fast offline neural isolation right on your Mac (Apple M1 Max runs it faster than real-time).
  • Local Open-Source (Demucs htdemucs): Runs locally with uvx demucs --two-stems=vocals input.wav to cleanly separate speech from any already-mixed track.
OPTION 3 · AT RECORDING TIME

Mic Placement, Gain Staging & Room Acoustics

Cost: $0 – $150 Impact: Permanent Fix Best for Future Shoots
  • Hit -12 to -6 dBFS on Camera Meters: Raise preamp/wireless transmitter gain until normal speech peaks around -10 dB (never -28 dB). Higher signal-to-noise ratio means cleaner post-processing.
  • Lav Placement: Clip center-chest 6–8 inches below your chin, pointing slightly upward, with a small cable loop taped under the collar to prevent clothing rub.
  • Upgrade to a Boom / Shotgun or 32-Bit Float Recorder: A hypercardioid boom mic (e.g. Deity S-Mic 2S / Rode NTG) just out of frame above your head, or a 32-bit float wireless lav (Rode Wireless Pro / DJI Mic 2), eliminates clipping and lav chest-resonance completely.
  • Kill Room Reverb ($0): Put a rug on the floor and hang a moving blanket or curtains on the wall behind the camera where your voice projects.
OPTION 4 · AUTOMATED CLI MASTERING

Broadcast FFmpeg Voice Chain (Zero Cuts, Phone-Ready)

Cost: $0 Impact: Medium–High 100% Automated

Avoid aggressive agate noise gates (which chop quiet syllables). Use gentle EQ + smooth compression + YouTube Shorts/Reels loudness normalization (-14 LUFS): 

ffmpeg -i voice_raw.wav -af "\
highpass=f=85,\
equalizer=f=220:t=q:w=1.2:g=-2.5,\
equalizer=f=2400:t=q:w=1.1:g=3.0,\
equalizer=f=6500:t=q:w=1.5:g=-2.0,\
acompressor=threshold=-22dB:ratio=2.5:attack=12:release=220:makeup=6dB,\
loudnorm=I=-14:TP=-1.0:LRA=7" \
-ar 48000 -c:a aac -b:a 192k voice_mastered.m4a
OPTION 5 · MUSIC SELECTION & EQ POCKETING

Make the Music Support the Voice Instead of Fighting It

Cost: $0 Impact: Instant Clarity
  • Carve a Vocal Pocket in the Music: Apply equalizer=f=2200:t=q:w=0.9:g=-5 only to the music track so the 1.5–3.5 kHz speech clarity band stays completely open for your voice.
  • Target -22 dB Under Speech: Keep background music around -36 to -38 LUFS while you speak (while voice sits at -14 to -15 LUFS). On phone speakers, anything louder competes directly with speech.
  • Prompt Minimal Instrumental Beds: When generating with ACE-Step, ask for "minimal warm ambient sub-bass pulse, sparse soft synth pads, no lead melody, no drums, low energy underscore".
